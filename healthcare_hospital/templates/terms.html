Patient Care Guidelines, Admission Protocols & Billing Terms

Home / Terms of Service

1. Clinical Consultation & Medical Protocol

Online appointment bookings, clinical summaries, and electronic records provided via this portal are designed to facilitate seamless patient care. In acute life-threatening situations, patients must immediately report to our 24/7 Emergency & Trauma Pavilion or dial our emergency helpline directly.

2. Appointments, Cancellations & Rescheduling

Patients are requested to arrive 15 minutes prior to their scheduled consultation slot. Cancellations or rescheduling can be managed online through the Appointments Portal without penalty up to 2 hours prior to the slot.

3. Billing & Fee Settlements

All professional fees, diagnostics, and prescription medication charges are itemized transparently. Digital receipts issued through the hospital portal serve as official tax-compliant proofs of medical settlement.
